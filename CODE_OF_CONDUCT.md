# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy technical community grounded in empirical evidence and respectful peer review.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Citing verifiable primary sources, empirical sample sizes, and reproducible methodologies
- Focusing constructively on technical accuracy and engineering trade-offs
- Respecting differing viewpoints and disclosures across open-source and commercial contributors

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or derogatory comments
- Undisclosed astroturfing, affiliate link injection, or adversarial spam
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers at `support@innflows.com`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
